📚 How to Use ByteRescue - A Beginner's Guide

Analyzing Your Drives

When you first open ByteRescue, you'll see a list of all connected drives. Simply click on a drive to scan it. The scan may take a few seconds to a few minutes depending on the size of your drive. Once complete, you'll see a visual breakdown of your files organized by type, size, and location.

Recovering Deleted Files

To recover deleted files, select the "Recover" option from the main menu. Choose the drive where you deleted the files, then click "Start Recovery". ByteRescue will scan for recoverable data and present a list of files you can restore. Simply check the files you want and click "Recover Selected".

Creating a Disk Image

If you want to create a complete backup of a drive, use the "Disk Imaging" feature. Select the source drive and choose a destination location for the image file. ByteRescue will create a complete sector-by-sector copy, which you can use for backup or forensic purposes.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is ByteRescue really free?

Yes! ByteRescue is completely free and open-source. You can use it for personal or commercial purposes without any cost or licensing restrictions.

Will ByteRescue damage my files?

No. ByteRescue only reads data from your drives during analysis and recovery. It does not modify or write to your drives unless you specifically choose to recover or save files to a new location.

Can I recover files from a formatted drive?

In many cases, yes. ByteRescue can often recover data from formatted drives as long as the data hasn't been overwritten. The sooner you try recovery after formatting, the better your chances.

Do I need to install anything else?

No. ByteRescue comes with everything you need. There are no additional dependencies or required installations.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Tips

⚠️ If ByteRescue doesn't start: Make sure your Windows is up to date. If you're using Windows 10, ensure you have the latest updates installed. You may also need to temporarily disable antivirus software that might be blocking the application.

⚠️ If recovery doesn't find deleted files: Stop using the drive immediately after deletion to prevent overwriting. The more you use the drive, the less likely you'll recover deleted files. Try recovery as soon as possible.

💡 For best results: Run ByteRescue as an administrator by right-clicking the application and selecting "Run as administrator". This ensures ByteRescue has full access to all drives.
